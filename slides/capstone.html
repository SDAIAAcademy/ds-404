Format

Ten minutes to defend the decision, ten minutes of questions from two assessors

1 · The task and the decision

Which task, who decides, what is decided. One slide.

2 · The protocol

The tuned baseline and its budget, two pretrained candidates, the ensemble, the validation scheme and the shift it matches.

3 · Results and conditions

Every number with its interval and its run; regret where a decision follows; the adoption decision with its conditions.

Peer feedback. After each defence, everyone writes one strength, one question and one suggestion.

Keep time strictly: ten minutes, then questions. The case data is Bustan's, synthetic; a participant may use another public structured dataset if the shift and the decision are stated as clearly.

Inside the ten minutes of questions

An assessor names a new shift; you predict which result changes before you re-run

Scenarioe.g. a third region, a supplier change, Ramadan inside the test window
→
Predictionwhich validation number moves, and which way
→
Re-runthe protocol on the new split, live or right after
What earns the marks. A prediction with a reason, then an honest comparison with the re-run, whichever way it went.

Assessors: choose a scenario the repository can re-run in minutes, such as holding out Tabuk, moving the temporal cut before Ramadan 1447, or dropping one segment from training. Score the reasoning under "Validation under shift", not whether the prediction was right.
